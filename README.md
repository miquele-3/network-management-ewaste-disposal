# Management and Disposal of Computing Equipment: Advanced IT Extension Project

**Advanced Academic Extension Project — FMU (Module: Advanced Applied Information Technology - 24.1)**

## 📌 Project Overview
An advanced IT implementation project focused on small business infrastructure optimization and ecological e-waste disposal. The project tackles local network management, hardware diagnostic/maintenance, and proper disposal of obsolete electronic equipment.

## 🎯 Alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
- **SDG 4:** Quality Education

## 💡 Key Contributions & Technical Scope
- **Network Management & Infrastructure:** Optimization of local small business networks, hardware updates, and power supply backup setup (generators/UPS).
- **Hardware Maintenance & Triage:** Diagnostic and separation of obsolete computational equipment for proper recycling.
- **Eco-friendly E-Waste Disposal:** Routing hazardous e-waste to official municipal collection points to mitigate environmental impact.
- **Tech Onboarding for Seniors:** Basic technical instruction for elderly staff and business owners to increase operational autonomy.
